Release Note

COPY LINK

emiprotechnologies.com/r/gg5

1. What is the scope of this connector? +
It is intended to cover core Shopify and Odoo operations including catalog, inventory, orders, fulfillment, returns, refunds, and payout-related workflows for standard usage.
2. Who is this connector for? +
It is for businesses running Shopify and Odoo that want better operational control, cleaner backend workflows, and more reliable live usage.
3. Is implementation included? +
Guided implementation support may be included for standard workflows where applicable. It is intended to help activation and go-live readiness, not replace full consulting.
4. Does it include custom workflow support? +
No. Custom workflows, non-standard business requirements, and customization work are outside standard connector scope unless separately planned.
5. How does support work after purchase? +
Support is handled through a structured support system intended for issue resolution, standard workflow guidance, and product troubleshooting. Unlimited support for the latest 3 versions - no time limit.
6. Is it compatible with multiple Shopify stores or companies? +
Yes. The connector is intended to support multi-store scenarios and can be aligned to multi-company or structured ERP environments depending on setup.
7. Can I try it before buying? +
If applicable, yes - the evaluation path can include a 15-day free trial so you can validate workflow fit before deeper commitment.
8. What should I prepare before implementation? +
You should ideally have reasonable clarity around products, inventory structure, taxes, accounting setup, warehouse logic, and expected business process.
9. Does it handle standard live workflows or just sync data? +
The product is positioned around operational usage, not just sync visibility. That means the real question is how it supports daily workflows in Odoo.
10. Why should maturity matter if features look similar elsewhere? +
Because what breaks after purchase usually is not visible in a feature list. Mature products tend to behave better under real business conditions.
11. Do the Payout Import feature support orders placed through Shopify Markets? +
Because It depends on your Shopify Markets configuration in Odoo: If all your markets are mapped to a single company in Odoo, the Payout Import works as expected and orders are reconciled normally. If your markets are mapped to multiple companies in Odoo, the Payout Import is not supported. Orders won't be correctly matched or reconciled during payout import.
